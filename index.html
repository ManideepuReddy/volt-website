Volters

A coin you can only mint by charging.

Volters are created when real energy goes into a real car. We call this proof of charge. Each ADDA charger signs its meter readings with its own key, VOLT checks that signature, and only then are volters minted. The rules are public: 10 volters per kWh, split three ways.

  1. MeasuredThe charger meters every kWh it delivers.
  2. SignedIt signs those readings with a key that never leaves the charger.
  3. VerifiedVOLT checks the signature against that charger.
  4. Minted10 volters per verified kWh.

Franchise

50,000volters

No franchise fee. Bring 50,000 volters.

To open a VOLT ADDA, a new franchise owner collects 50,000 volters instead of paying us a fee. Most will buy them on open crypto markets, from the drivers and home-charger owners who earned them. The money a franchise would usually pay head office goes back to the people who charge with VOLT.

Mine volters from your own parking spot.
