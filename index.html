Lightnings

A coin you can only create by charging.

Lightnings are VOLT's crypto. Charging at a Volt Adda is the only way new Lightnings are created: our Volt Box measures and signs every kWh, the signature is checked, and only then are Lightnings minted to you. Hold them, or trade them once trading opens.

  1. MeasuredThe Volt Box meters every kWh the charger delivers.
  2. SignedIt signs those readings with a key that never leaves the box.
  3. VerifiedVOLT checks the signature. That is proof of charge.
  4. MintedLightnings land in your wallet.
